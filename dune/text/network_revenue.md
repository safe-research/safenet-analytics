## Network & revenue

Safenet production only (Beta was a separate network and is not included). Counters and the window table are **all time unless stated**, and charts show the **last 30 days** (UTC). Activity counts Safes on the Charter's networks (Ethereum, Gnosis Chain, Arbitrum One), including Safenet's own operational Safes. Requests for Safes on other networks are likely tests and are only listed separately. Amounts are in **USDC.e**, the token fees are paid in, with no USD values. *Data as of* shows how fresh the data is.
