### Realized staking APR (completed reward periods)

What staking earned in each **completed** two-week Aegis reward period (from 2026-10-13), from the published rewards and the stake that actually earned them. **History only**: not current stake, not an estimate of future returns. Rewards are those [published on chain](https://github.com/safe-research/safenet-analytics/blob/main/docs/metrics/staking.md#realized-apr) by the ENS name `rewards.safenet-gov.eth` and the distributor's Merkle root. A period that has ended but is not published yet is listed without rewards or APR, never as 0.
